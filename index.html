How matching works. Nationality and degree level decide eligibility. Bachelor's applicants are scored on HSC GPA (30), field (25), English (25) and SSC GPA (20). Master's applicants on field (25), Bachelor's CGPA (25), English (20), work experience (15) and research record (15). PhD applicants on Master's CGPA (20), publications (20), field (15), English (15), Bachelor's CGPA (10), research proposal (10) and supervisor (10). A missed compulsory requirement caps the score at 49.

Best match order. The list is sorted by match score, highest first. When scores are equal, the more competitive scholarship comes first, then the nearer deadline. Scholarships you are not eligible for are always at the end.

Check before you rely on it. The scholarships are real programmes, but eligible countries, months, and the SSC, HSC, CGPA, IELTS and publication levels on this page are indicative values that have not been checked against the official websites. Programmes change every year. Verify each entry before you publish. Saved scholarships and ticked documents stay in this browser only.
